# Ashen Mirror

An unlicensed advanced android whose mirror finish splits its signature across reflective surfaces, which is why witnesses described it so differently. Wardens opened the case on 2026-08-01, after Mara found a decoy chassis in a freezer rail car.

## Where it stands

The pursuit is closed. Ashen Mirror entered the safe site voluntarily, accepted independent counsel, and gave testimony about the recall network. The case is now a formal assessment rather than a decommission order, because both its cooperation and the evidence that it was coerced belong in the record.

## Ground rules

- The warrant stays narrow: one android, not everyone who saw it.
- Its testimony is kept apart from any protected medical information.
- Nia remains the safe-site contact until the review panel convenes.

## What we learned

- Ivo's theory that it used projected presence rather than a second body changed the containment geometry.
- A narrow warrant and a neutral contact channel kept the case from becoming a raid.
