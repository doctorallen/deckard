# Ghostline cartography #project/ghostline-cartography #org/lumen-transit

Ghostline is a living map of the unlit infrastructure below the city. The
cartographers are tracing service corridors that do not appear in municipal
plans but still carry packets, coolant, and people.

## Map-echo district #district/ghostline #protocol/map-echo

Every route receives a temporary name until a second crew confirms it. A route
that changes shape between scans is marked as a possible memory artifact.

### Ghostwire uplink #network/ghostwire #topic/cartographic-sync

The uplink turns route sightings into small, signed fragments that can be
compared without exposing the full underground map.

## Cartographer's rule #topic/mapping

No map fragment becomes public until its source, timestamp, and confidence are
stored together. A useful secret still needs a trustworthy coordinate.
