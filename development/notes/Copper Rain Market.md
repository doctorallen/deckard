# Copper Rain market #project/copper-rain #org/night-market-union

Copper Rain is the market's agreement to share enough inventory data to keep
food, batteries, and clean filters moving during a transit disruption. Nobody
wants a central ledger, so the stalls trade narrow proofs instead.

## Vendor-shield district #district/copper-rain #protocol/vendor-shield

The market's first shield is social: each stall can revoke one compromised
credential without taking its neighbors offline.

### Ghostwire uplink #network/ghostwire #topic/market-coordination

Ghostwire carries the small proofs between stalls and gives the market a
common order for resolving two claims on the same crate.

## Stall accord #topic/market-ops

The market will publish a shortage notice only after three independent stalls
confirm the same missing shipment. Panic is treated as another kind of leak.
