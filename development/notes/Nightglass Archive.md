# Nightglass archive #project/nightglass-archive #org/memory-lattice

Nightglass preserves fragments that the city would otherwise forget: failed
evacuations, erased street names, and the small decisions that kept people
alive during the first network fracture.

## Mirror vault district #district/nightglass #protocol/mirror-vault

The archive maintains two copies with different custodians. A perfect match is
treated as suspicious when the custodians used the same source terminal.

### Ghostwire uplink #network/ghostwire #topic/archive-sync

Ghostwire carries provenance proofs between the vault and the active projects,
but never carries an unredacted memory fragment.

#### Blue static index #signal/blue-static #risk/duplicate-memory

Several fragments share a checksum while describing different nights. The
archive may have copied a reconstruction as if it were eyewitness material.

- [ ] Compare capture clocks before merging the fragments.
- [ ] Ask @sera-nox to mark the uncertain index entries.

#### Paper moon custodian #operator/kenji-rook #artifact/paper-moon

Kenji has the only paper register for the oldest vault rooms. Its margins
contain route notes that do not exist in the digital catalog.

- [ ] Scan the route notes without exposing the room numbers.
- [ ] Link the register to the Ghostline cartographic confidence record.

## Provenance rule #topic/archival-integrity

The archive keeps uncertainty visible. A fragment may be useful before it is
trusted, but it must never lose the label that explains why.
