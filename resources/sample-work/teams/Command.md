# Command

The captain and contracts: which jobs the Petrel takes, where it goes, and
the paperwork at every port. Its people are the notes whose `team:` names
it; the team's page lists them as **members**.

## Open work on the team

Everything still open in the notes of the people on this team. `this` is
the note the block is in, so the same block works in any team's note.

```deckard
team = this AND is:open
```
