# Buoy checklist #contract/relay-survey

What happens to every buoy of the [[Relay survey]] before and during the EVA
that sets it. Dev keeps this list.

1. On the bench: a fresh battery pack, a beacon test, and the housing sealed.
2. Before the EVA: two people suited, one inside on the clock, and the RCS
   held so the ship does not drift.
3. Outside: set the buoy, wait for its beacon, and read back its position.
4. After: log the EVA and send the co-op the position.
