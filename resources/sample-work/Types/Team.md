# Team

| Field   | Kind   | Reverse | Also called  | Words                           |
| ------- | ------ | ------- | ------------ | ------------------------------- |
| lead    | Person | lead of | head, chief  | leads, led by, now leads, heads |
| channel | Text   |         | comms, radio |                                 |

Every `#team/…` tag is one of the Petrel's teams. Its fields live in its hub
note, such as [[Engineering]]. A line such as "@dev-laurent now leads
#team/engineering" offers, on its lightbulb, to set Engineering's lead: the
Words column says which phrases mean which field.
