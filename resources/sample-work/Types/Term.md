# Term

| Field | Kind | Reverse | Also called           |
| ----- | ---- | ------- | --------------------- |
| term  | Text |         | acronym, abbreviation |

A short term the notes use, such as [[3DS]]. A term note's front matter
says `type: term` and `term:` the term, and its first paragraph is what
the term means. Hover the term in any note to read it there.
