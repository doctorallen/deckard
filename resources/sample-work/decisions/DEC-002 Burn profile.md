# DEC-002 Burn profile #decision #contract/relay-survey

## Context
On leg one, each long burn left the survey array out of true for hours,
and the co-op pays for survey time, not for waiting.

## Decision
Two short burns per leg instead of one long one, so the survey array
settles between them. A TCM after the second burn when it leaves us off
the line.

## Consequences
- A little more fuel per leg; Juno checked the margin.
- The array settles in under an hour. Leg two proved it; see the
  [[Survey flight plan]].
