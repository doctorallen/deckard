# DEC-001 Survey route #decision #contract/relay-survey

## Context
Twelve buoys, three legs, and a co-op that wants the far buoys working
first.

## Decision
Fly the far leg first, while the burn windows are wide, and work back
toward Pallas Yard, putting in there between legs two and three.

## Consequences
- A stop at Pallas Yard midway, for fuel and anything that needs fixing.
- The last leg is the shortest, so a slip there costs the least.
