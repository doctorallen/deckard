## Notes are ordinary Markdown

Deckard reads the files you already keep. Nothing is moved, and nothing is
sent anywhere.

```markdown
# 2026-09-17

## Harbor check-in #team/harbor
Sable and Rhea found the cache. Counsel signs off on Friday.

- [ ] Send the warrant packet to counsel 📅 2026-09-20
```

A heading, a tag, and a checklist item are all Deckard needs.
