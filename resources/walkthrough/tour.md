## A guided walk through Deckard

The tour runs on the work sample: six weeks aboard the Petrel, a small
survey and hauling ship.

- Today's note, tags, and people
- Tasks, the Task board, and the calendar
- Types as tables and boards, and hub notes

Each scene opens a real page beside Help and gives you one thing to try.
Present runs it, for showing Deckard to someone.
